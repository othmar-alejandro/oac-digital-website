# Homepage copy · draft 4

Phase 1. Positioning: **AI agency that sells results.** Websites are one of ten services. Voice is "we". EN and ES for every section. `[OTHMAR]` marks a fact only you have.

Frame used (Hormozi's value equation, Brunson's hook, story, offer): lead with the result the owner wants, show it's believable with real work, make it sound fast and low effort for them. No invented numbers anywhere.

The three results everything hangs on, taken from your own words: **get more clients · keep them coming back · stay organized.**

One ask on the whole page: **get a free consultation**. No prices anywhere on the page.
